AI for Engineering

I study how AI can help engineers interpret sensor data and make better decisions about operating, inspecting, and maintaining infrastructure. My work spans renewable energy, water, and oil and gas systems.

Research areasSelected publications

Research focus

Where I apply AI

Across these systems, the goal is to connect measurements and physical models to decisions people can evaluate: what to inspect, when to intervene, and how to measure the result.

Renewable energy systems

AI for renewable hydrogen operations, from electrolyzer dispatch and storage to leak response, maintenance, and sustainability assessment.

Water systems

Sensor placement and data analysis for water, wastewater, and stormwater infrastructure to improve monitoring and guide inspection priorities.

Oil and gas systems

AI-assisted pipeline inspection and integrity assessment using guided-wave signals, experiments, and models of damage and degradation.
